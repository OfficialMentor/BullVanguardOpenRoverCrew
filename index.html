Who We Are

Bull Vanguard Rover Scouts #044 MIG

The Bull Vanguard Scouts Rover Open Crew (#044 MIG) is an elite self-governing unit of young adult scouts (ages 18-26) dedicated to high-adventure exploration, active citizenship, and personal mastery.
